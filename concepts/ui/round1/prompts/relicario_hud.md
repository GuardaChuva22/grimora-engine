# Direction: RELICÁRIO (Reliquary), in-game HUD. File name: relicario_hud.png

Inspiration: gothic reliquaries and cathedral metalwork. Materials: pitted black iron, yellowed
bone, ultramarine and blood red stained-glass enamel, tiny rivets. Heavy, sacred, a little
disturbing, like objects kept in a crypt. Surreal touches: a small eye set into the metal, ribs
used as brackets.

Keep the attached game screenshot unchanged as the full frame, and add only this HUD on top:
- Bottom left: a compact black iron reliquary holding a blood filled glass vein (health) and a thinner pale bone-coloured vein (stamina), with a small socketed eye at its end. Labels "Vida" and "Fôlego", with "84/120" beside the health.
- Bottom centre: a row of 6 small iron framed niches with bone corners; slot 1 selected with a lit stained-glass ultramarine rim. Niches contain small pixel art items: straight sword, flintlock pistol, red apple, potion, black book, empty. Small numerals 1 to 6. The apple shows "x3".
- Bottom right: a small round iron monstrance holding the current spell icon (a shard of ice) behind enamel, with the label "Lança de Gelo".
- Top centre: a slim iron compass bar with the letters N and L, and one small marker.
- Centre lower: an interaction prompt on a small iron plaque: key "E" in a bone box, then "Abrir baú".
- Left, above the reliquary: a short pickup note: "+1 Maçã".
Keep the pieces small, so the world stays the star.
