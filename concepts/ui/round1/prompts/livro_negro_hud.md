# Direction: LIVRO NEGRO (Black Book), in-game HUD. File name: livro_negro_hud.png

Inspiration: the Black Hours, medieval manuscripts on black-dyed parchment with gold and silver
leaf. Materials: black vellum, burnished worn gold leaf, ultramarine pigment, a touch of vermilion
ink. Thin hand-ruled lines, small sigils, marginal flourishes. The interface looks inked and gilded
straight onto the darkness. Restrained: on the field the HUD almost disappears.

Keep the attached game screenshot unchanged as the full frame, and add only this HUD on top:
- Bottom left: health and stamina as two thin gilded ruled lines with vermilion ink (health) and pale gold ink (stamina) filling them, with a small sigil at the start of each. Labels "Vida" and "Fôlego", with "84/120" beside the health.
- Bottom centre: a row of 6 small slots shaped like illuminated initial boxes, ruled in thin gold on black vellum; slot 1 selected with a brighter double gold rule. Slots contain small pixel art items: straight sword, flintlock pistol, red apple, potion, black book, empty. Small numerals 1 to 6. The apple shows "x3".
- Bottom right: a small round gilded seal holding the current spell icon (a shard of ice), with the label "Lança de Gelo".
- Top centre: a thin ruled compass line with the letters N and L, and one small gold marker.
- Centre lower: an interaction prompt on a small torn strip of black vellum: key "E" in a gold box, then "Abrir baú".
- Left, above the bars: a short pickup note in gold ink: "+1 Maçã".
Keep it sparse: the world stays the star.
