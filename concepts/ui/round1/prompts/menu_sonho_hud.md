# Direction: MENU DE SONHO (Dream Menu), in-game HUD. File name: menu_sonho_hud.png

Inspiration: real 1990s PlayStation RPG interfaces such as King's Field, Vagrant Story, Shadow
Tower and Silent Hill: translucent dark panels with a stepped dithered fade, hairline pale borders,
small caps pixel type, numbers shown plainly, unsettling minimalism. Almost no ornament; the
strangeness comes from one or two surreal marks (a small open eye, a moon phase).

Keep the attached game screenshot unchanged as the full frame, and add only this HUD on top:
- Bottom left: "VIDA" and "FÔLEGO" in small caps with the numbers "84/120" and "61/100", each with a hairline bar, on a dithered translucent dark panel. A small moon phase glyph shows the hour.
- Bottom centre: a row of 6 small square slots with hairline borders; slot 1 selected with an inverted pale border. Slots contain small low-poly, pre-rendered looking items: straight sword, flintlock pistol, red apple, potion, black book, empty. Small numerals 1 to 6. The apple shows "x3".
- Bottom right: a small square holding the current spell icon (a shard of ice), with the label "LANÇA DE GELO".
- Top centre: a hairline compass strip with the letters N and L, and one small marker.
- Centre lower: an interaction prompt on a dithered strip: key "E" in a hairline box, then "ABRIR BAÚ".
- Left, above the panel: a short pickup note: "+1 MAÇÃ".
Keep it sparse: the world stays the star.
