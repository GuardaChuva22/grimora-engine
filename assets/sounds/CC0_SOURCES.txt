These files are recordings dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0 Universal,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They were trimmed, made mono,
matched in loudness to the sounds they replaced, and loops were given a seamless seam.
Every other file in this folder is made by tools/gen_sounds.py.

Collections they came from:
  - "500 Game Sound Effects" (CC0 compilation; per-file sources below)
  - "RPG Audio" by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl), CC0
